Controls

  • Mouse Aim, hold left click to fire
  • R Reload. Tap again in the green zone for a Perfect reload
  • 1 2 3 Q Switch weapons
  • Space E Gadgets
  • Esc Pause  ·  M Mute

About the project

Hold a crumbling sea wall for twenty nights. Enemies crawl out of the surf wet and dry off as they cross the sand, so where you kill them matters.

A wall-defence game in the style of the old Flash classics The Last Stand and Bunny Invasion. It has 8 weapons, 9 mods, gadgets, beach traps, a hireable crew, tide cards that trade risk for scrap, and four boss fights.

Written in plain JavaScript with no libraries. All the art is drawn in code and every sound is synthesized with the Web Audio API. I balanced it with a headless bot that plays full 20-night campaigns.
